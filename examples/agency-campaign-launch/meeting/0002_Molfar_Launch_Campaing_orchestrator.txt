# Molfar System Kit: 4-week launch plan (Campaign Project Manager)

I used no web search this round. Everything below comes from the participants' answers and the three brief documents. Where a claim comes only from a participant's own page fetch, I say so.

## Goal

The brief sets the direction, and the owner sets the final numbers:
- People find the Kit and install packs.
- A few outsiders react: a star, a comment, a question or a submitted role.
- Molfar gets new installs from the Kit.
- The owner ends with a routine that fits 4 hours a week and survives a lost week.

The Analytics Reporter proposed "worked" thresholds as judgment bands, not growth targets. They are in the Week 4 table, and the owner decides whether to adopt them.

## Shared base (before any channel step)

- **Message.** Use the Content Creator's one-sentence and one-paragraph descriptions, announcement text and 5-question FAQ as the single source. Every channel reuses them. Both the Content Creator and the Twitter Engager built these from `02_facts.md`.
- **Tagging.** Use one campaign name, `kit-launch`, with the Analytics Reporter's tagged website link. GitHub links cannot carry a UTM.
- **Disclosure.** Every post says the author made the project: a solo developer from Ukraine, working with Claude. The brief requires this.

**Cut or deferred:**

- Facebook and Hacker News get no steps, because nobody proposed any.
- A Ukrainian-language wave stays pending the owner's answer.

## Week-4 thresholds (the Analytics Reporter's proposal)

- **Worked:** at least 3 of these 4 are true.
  - 14-day unique visitors reach at least 30, and at least 3 times the day-1 figure.
  - Net new stars are at least 5.
  - There are at least 3 outside replies.
  - Play acquisitions are at least 5 above the prior 28 days, or at least 3 acquisitions carry a campaign UTM.
- **Did not work:** only if all of these hold:
  - No 14-day window is more than 10 visitors above day 1.
  - Net stars are under 2.
  - There are no outside replies.
  - Play acquisitions are not above the prior 28 days.
- **Unclear:** everything else, including a lost GitHub window.

A pack install inside the app cannot be measured. The app has no analytics, and GitHub does not count raw-file downloads. Proxies are unique cloners, questions about the install steps, and submitted roles.

## If a week is lost

- **Week 2 lost:** steps 6–8 simply move or are dropped. The Twitter Engager's rule is that a missed week is dropped, not repeated. The launch assets from week 1 keep working.
- **Weeks 1–2 lost before launch:** everything shifts. Step 1 (the baseline) still has to come before the first post.
- **Offline after launch for 15 days or more:** GitHub keeps only 14 days of traffic and has no backfill, so early days are lost for good. The Analytics Reporter says to record a blank cell as "lost", not zero. The weekly check is the only safeguard, so take a snapshot on launch day.
- **Unanswered replies:** the Twitter Engager points calls to action at Discussions so answers stay findable. The Reddit Community Builder gave a reply template for Reddit only. **Nobody addressed who answers GitHub Discussions while the owner is offline.**

## Forks (owner's call)

- **Reddit.**
  - *Branch A, skip it for these 4 weeks.* This is the Reddit Community Builder's own headline conclusion. It costs nothing, and it avoids the risks of a shadowbanned old account, new-account filters and a possible ban on AI-assisted text.
  - *Branch B, warm-up.* About 1 hour a week of the 4 hours, plus the risks above. Comment-only in weeks 1–2, a post in weeks 3–4 only if the account is in good standing.
  - The Reddit Community Builder's answer is internally split between these two. Their sources are third-party guides, not Reddit's own pages, so sidebars must be checked first.
  - A cheap option within Branch A: check the old account's profile while logged out (a few minutes) and decide on one appeal.
- **Website fixes.** The Content Creator reports from the live site that screenshots are "coming soon", that the role limit reads 10,000 characters where the facts file says 25,000, and that no Kit link is obvious on the homepage. I have not verified this myself. Nobody owns or estimates the fixes. Whether the owner can do them from a phone is open.
