### 3. Four-week rhythm — ~1 hour a week

One sitting per week, one post per week. Batch-write everything in the first sitting (40 min), keep it in a phone note, so a bad week costs nothing.

| Week | Post (pre-written) | Reply time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Launch thread (above) | 20 min |
| 2 | "Role of the week" — one role from a pack, what it does, which pack | 20 min |
| 3 | Install walk-through: the five steps from post 5/ | 20 min |
| 4 | "Submit a role without git" — the issue form, and what we do with it | 20 min |

Rules that make it survive a lost week: never post twice in one day to "catch up"; a missed week is simply dropped, not rescheduled; no post promises a date or a follow-up. Only the launch thread and the four singles are ever published — nothing depends on being online that day.
