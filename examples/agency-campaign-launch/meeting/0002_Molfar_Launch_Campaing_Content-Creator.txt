## 4. Newcomer FAQ

Source: `02_facts.md`, “Molfar System” and “Molfar System Kit.”

1. **What is a role?** A prompt describes who the model is and how it talks; a skill describes what it does and in what order.
2. **What do I need?** An Android device running Android 7.0 or newer, Molfar 2.0.5 or newer for pack installation, and your own AI provider key.
3. **Does installing a pack delete my roles?** No. Identical roles are skipped; for a changed role, you can replace it, keep both, or skip it.
4. **Where do my requests go?** Requests go directly from your phone to your chosen provider. Molfar has no server of its own.
5. **Can I contribute without git?** Yes. Use the repository’s New issue → “Submit a role” form.
