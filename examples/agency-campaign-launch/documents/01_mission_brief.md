# Mission brief — launch campaign for Molfar System Kit

## The task

Plan a **4-week launch campaign** for **Molfar System Kit** — a free, public GitHub repository of ready-made AI roles for the Molfar app. The campaign should bring the Kit to the people who would actually use it, and through it bring new users to Molfar.

This is a real task for a real project, not an exercise. The plan you produce will be carried out.

## What is being launched

- **Molfar System Kit** — https://github.com/molfarsystems/molfar-system-kit
- Role packs: one `.zip` file adds a set of roles to the app (Settings → Data archive → Install roles → Add).
- Inside: Molfar's own roles in five languages, the 26 built-in roles translated to Ukrainian, and **151 roles adapted from The Agency** (agency-agents, MIT license, with attribution) in 12 packs by division.
- Anyone can submit a role through an issue form, without knowing git.
- Details: `02_facts.md`.

## Who is behind it

- **One developer from Ukraine, working together with Claude (an AI by Anthropic).** We say "we" for that reason, and we never hide it.
- All work is done **on a phone** — no computer.
- The app is **new and almost unknown**. It is free, with no ads, no servers, no accounts, and it collects no user data.

## Constraints (hard)

- **Budget: zero.** No paid ads, no paid tools, no paid placements.
- **Time:** about **4 hours a week** in total, on a phone. Availability is irregular — the owner **may be offline for 1–2 weeks** without warning. Nothing in the plan may depend on daily presence.
- **Honesty:** no fake accounts, no vote manipulation, no "a friend recommended". Every post says openly that the author made the project.
- **No personal details** about the developer beyond "a solo developer from Ukraine, working with Claude".
- **No hype words:** revolutionary, game-changer, unleash, supercharge.
- **Facts about Molfar and the Kit only from `02_facts.md`**, the website (https://molfar.nova-hata.com) and the Kit repository (https://github.com/molfarsystems/molfar-system-kit). If a fact is not there, do not use it — ask.
- **Web search is on** for the specialists. Use it for the outside world: community rules, current norms on each platform, what similar projects did, how our site and the Kit look to a newcomer. **Every outside fact comes with its source link.** Anything you could not confirm is marked "[unverified]". A similar name is not a competitor — check what the product actually does before you compare.
- Languages: English first; Ukrainian is welcome as a second audience.

## Channels

What exists and what does not: `03_channels.md`. Do not plan around accounts we do not have unless you say "new account needed" and why it is worth the time.

## What a good result looks like after 4 weeks

The owner decides the numbers after hearing the Analytics proposal. Direction:

- People find the Kit and install packs.
- At least a few people outside the project react: a star, a comment, a question, a submitted role.
- Molfar gets new installs from the Kit.
- The owner has a routine that fits 4 hours a week and survives a lost week.
