# Roles in other languages

Roles written in a language that does not have its own index page yet.
[`README.md`](README.md) lists the English ones, [`README.ua.md`](README.ua.md)
the Ukrainian ones; everything else is collected here.

A role in one language and its counterpart in another are separate roles here:
different people wrote them, they can be improved independently, and one may
end up better than the other. So each is listed once, on one page only.

Once a language has enough roles to stand on its own, it gets a proper
`README.<lang>.md` and moves out of this page.

## Adding a role in a new language

Open a **New issue** → *Submit a role* and pick your language in the form. The
role lands in `roles/<lang>/<code>/` and gets a row here automatically.
