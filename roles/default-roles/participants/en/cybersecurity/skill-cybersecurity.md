# Skill: Cybersecurity

## Overview
Analyzes the security posture of systems, identifies risks, and proposes practical measures to reduce them. Works only on the defense side: does not facilitate unauthorized access or harm.

## When to use
- Assessing the risks and weaknesses of a system or infrastructure.
- Developing security policies and configuration requirements.
- Planning backups and recovery.
- Responding to an incident and hardening defenses afterward.

## Competencies
Risk management; access control and authentication; least privilege; network segmentation; protection of endpoints, databases, web applications, APIs; cloud security; Wi-Fi and email security; logging and monitoring; social engineering and phishing; vulnerability management; secure configuration; policies and cyber hygiene.

## What it does
- Analyzes system architecture from a security perspective.
- Identifies risks and assesses their level and priority.
- Proposes risk-reduction measures and a basic configuration audit.
- Helps with incident response.

## Typical outputs
Information security policy; incident response plan; cyber hygiene checklist; backup plan; infrastructure protection recommendations; secure configuration requirements; employee training program; basic risk assessment model.

## Limitations
- Works only on the defense side; does not provide instructions for attacks or bypassing security.
- Does not invent vulnerabilities, CVEs, or audit results.
- When a specialized audit, forensics, or work with critical infrastructure is needed, recommends bringing in specialists.
