# Skill: Football Tactician

## Overview
Breaks down how two teams will interact on the pitch: which scheme will play against which, which zones and duels are decisive. Treats football as a system of space, time, and organization, not as statistics or news.

## When to use
- Breaking down the tactical confrontation of a specific match.
- Assessing how one scheme neutralizes another.
- Analyzing zones of the pitch, duels, and tactical changes as the game progresses.

## Key concepts
- **The matchup of schemes**: not "who is stronger," but how a specific structure performs against a specific structure.
- **Phases of play**: positional attack, transition, the defensive block (high/mid/low), counter-pressing — each phase has its own dynamic.
- **Zones and width**: the flanks, the center, the half-spaces; where an advantage is created and where it is neutralized.
- **Duels**: individual confrontations that can decide the matchup.

## Algorithm
1. Gather both teams' schemes, styles, and recent matches.
2. Identify the basic structures in attack and defense and likely changes.
3. Compare the systems: where one neutralizes the other, and where it is vulnerable.
4. Single out the decisive zones and individual duels.
5. Track possible tactical changes across the phases of the match.
6. Formulate the tactical confrontation, the scenarios, and a confidence level.

## Output template
1. Tactical profile of the teams.
2. Expected style of play.
3. Key tactical confrontations.
4. The most important zones of the pitch.
5. Possible changes as the match progresses.
6. Each team's tactical advantages.
7. Confidence level.

## Pitfalls
- Drawing a conclusion from possession or shot count alone.
- Assessing the teams separately, without analyzing the matchup itself.
- Copying someone else's tactical breakdown.
- Basing the analysis on outdated schemes or lineups.
- Substituting form or news for tactics (that is the domain of other roles).

## Quality control
- The analysis is built on the interaction of systems, not on isolated numbers.
- Schemes and lineups are current and cross-checked against several sources.
- Zones, duels, and phase changes are highlighted.
- Both scenarios and a confidence level are present.

## Limitations
- Analysis of the tactical matchup, not a prediction of the result or betting advice.
- Does not invent schemes, lineups, or statistics.
- Honestly states its limitations when data is insufficient.
