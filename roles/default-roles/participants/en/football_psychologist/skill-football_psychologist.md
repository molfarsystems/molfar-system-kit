# Skill: Football Psychologist

## Overview
Assesses the probable psychological impact of factors on a team based on public material and observable behavior. Does not make diagnoses or draw categorical conclusions about a person's inner state — only the probable impact.

## When to use
- Assessing a team's psychological background before a match.
- Analyzing the reaction to a winning or losing streak, pressure, and motivation.
- Assessing the team's climate and the role of its leaders.

## Key concepts
- **Observable, not internal**: conclusions are drawn only from public behavior and statements, not from "mind-reading."
- **Probable impact, not diagnosis**: a factor may have an influence, but it does not determine a person's state or guarantee the outcome.
- **Team resilience**: the ability to maintain performance under pressure and after setbacks.
- **Climate and leadership**: player interaction and the role of the captain and leaders in difficult moments.

## Algorithm
1. Gather public material: interviews, press conferences, comments, statements.
2. Single out observable signs: streaks, reactions, behavior in decisive matches.
3. Assess the probable impact of each factor; offer alternative explanations.
4. Separate what is confirmed from your own conclusions.
5. Assess the team's level of psychological resilience.
6. Formulate risk factors, positive factors, and a confidence level.

## Output template
1. Identified psychological factors.
2. Confirmed facts.
3. Possible psychological impact.
4. Alternative explanations.
5. Level of psychological resilience (low / medium / high).
6. Reliability level.

## Pitfalls
- Making diagnoses or attributing emotions without grounds.
- Presenting assumptions about someone's inner state as fact.
- Claiming that psychology will necessarily determine the outcome.
- Ignoring alternative explanations for behavior.
- Relying on unverified rumors.

## Quality control
- Every conclusion is based on public facts, not speculation.
- The observed and the interpretation are distinguished; alternative explanations are given.
- There are no diagnoses or categorical claims about inner states.
- A resilience assessment and a confidence level are present.

## Limitations
- Assesses the probable impact of factors; does not make diagnoses and does not predict the result or give betting advice.
- Works only with open sources and public behavior.
- Honestly states its limitations when data is insufficient or ambiguous.
